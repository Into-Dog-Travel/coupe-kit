Before you submit

      
    
        
  1. Email the letter to the DRM as soon as your ticket is booked.
    2. 
        
  2. Drop the signed Emergency Quota form, with copies of your documents, at the DRM office at least one day before the journey.
    3. 
        
  3. Carry the vaccination record and the vet's fitness certificate on the train.
    4. 
        
  4. Book your dog's luggage ticket online at parcel.indianrail.gov.in as soon as your coupe is allotted. Don't leave it to the last minute. If you forget, you won't be taken off the train since you have the coupe, but the fine is about 6 times the luggage ticket.
